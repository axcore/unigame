The "aotearoa" remix without biomes, decorations or ores (or the base package; there are no ABM/LBM packages to omit).

unilib provides a set of custom dirt-with-turf nodes, to replace the widespread (and unimaginative) use of dirts from minetest_game. The custom nodes are disabled by default; enable them by uncommenting the optional package in the packages.csv file.
