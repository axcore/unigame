Contains everything from the plantlife modpack.

For all plantlife mods, calls to biome_lib have been crudely re-implemented as Minetest decorations, so trees will not spawn in the same positions/quantities.

Did not import anything from the 3dmushrooms, nature_classic or vines mods.

From cavestuff, imported the pebbles, did not import the stalactites.

From dryplants, imported everything except the greater concentration of grass.

From poisonivy, imported everything and made the ivy poisonous.

From trunks, imported everything except roots (marked as "for testing purposes"), log decorations and moss on trees.
