Contains everything from the fantasy cheeses/effects.

General usage:

1. Boil milk in a cauldron to obtain curd (and whey)

2. Place the whey in a cheese rack, in a dark place, to obtain aged cheeses. Curd and whey can also be cooked in a furnace to produce a small range of cheeses

3. Craft stretched cheese from curd, water and salt, which produces cheeses like mozzarella. Of those, caciovallo must be hanged on a string (in a dark place) before it becomes eatable

4. Right-click a cream centrigue with either whey or milk to produce cream

5. Right-click a churn with either cream or milk to produce butter
